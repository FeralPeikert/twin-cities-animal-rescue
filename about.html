About Us

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit focused on helping animals find safe homes and connecting people with meaningful ways to help.

Our Story

Our work brings together adopters, foster families, volunteers, donors, and community partners who share a commitment to improving the lives of animals in need.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We approach animals and people with patience, kindness, and respect.

Responsibility

We support thoughtful animal placement and work toward safe, lasting homes.

Community

We believe lasting change happens when volunteers, families, and local partners work together.

A Message from Our Team

Community Partners

We appreciate the veterinary professionals, foster families, volunteers, donors, and community organizations that help support animals throughout the rescue process.
